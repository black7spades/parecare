**Viewpoint:** Support worker (Jess)

**Who:** Jess, doing the round for Margaret.

**Wants to:** Give doses, record an as needed dose, a refusal and a medicine that has run out.

**Must see or do:** Instructions and allergy visible, as needed rules, refusal alerts the family, out of stock is an outcome.

**Plan scenario:** 6

## Steps

1. Open the Medication record and see doses due.
2. Use "Log all due doses".
3. An allergy banner shows on the round.
4. Each row shows instructions and with food.
5. As needed dose asks a reason and shows last given, maximum and gap.
6. Record a refused dose.
7. A refused dose still raises the overdue alert.
8. The family is told it was refused.
9. Record "not in stock" as the outcome.
10. A contributor records a dose change or marks a reorder.
