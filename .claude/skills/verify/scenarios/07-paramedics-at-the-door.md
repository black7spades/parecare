# 07. Paramedics at the door: emergency sheet

**Viewpoint:** Daughter caring for her mum (Linda, Dorothy)

**Who:** Linda, handing the sheet to paramedics.

**Wants to:** One page with what they need, fast.

**Must see or do:** ID numbers, directive, emergency contact with phone, current conditions, tappable phones, shareable without login.

**Plan scenario:** 7

## Steps

1. Open the emergency sheet.
2. Medicare, IHI, DVA, NDIS and private health numbers appear.
3. The carer is listed as emergency contact with a phone.
4. The advance care directive is listed.
5. Phone numbers can be tapped.
6. Allergy severity shown.
7. Mobility, diet and swallowing needs shown.
8. Share by a link that needs no login.

## Baseline (before any fixes)

1. PASS. One page, print button, allergies, medicines, GP.
2. FAIL. There is nowhere to record them.
3. FAIL. "Not recorded". The daughter is not offered either.
4. FAIL. Margaret has a directive document. Her sheet does not mention it.
5. FAIL. No phone number on the sheet is a link.
6. FAIL. Reaction only.
7. FAIL. No such section.
8. FAIL. Output only.
