# 05. Staff shift start and handover

**Viewpoint:** Support worker (Jess)

**Who:** Jess, support worker on contributor access. Login worker@bench.test.

**Wants to:** Start a shift and know who needs what, what happened last and who to call.

**Must see or do:** Allergies, GP, emergency contact, meds due, care needs, last handover with its author, what changed since last time.

**Plan scenario:** 5

## Steps

1. Sign in and see who needs attention.
2. Open a resident and see allergies, GP and emergency contact at once.
3. See the last handover and who wrote it.
4. See what changed since last time.
5. The card phone number says whose phone it is.

## Baseline (before any fixes)

1. PASS. Home lists Dorothy and six residents with an attention list of 7 things.
2. FAIL. Margaret Hughes has a Penicillin allergy and a daughter on file. Overview shows neither. The emergency sheet says contacts "Not recorded".
3. FAIL. The handover is mixed into the care log card. The log stores no author for it.
4. PASS. Cards say "new since your last visit".
5. FAIL. The card says "Phone" and shows the GP number for Dorothy and the next of kin number for residents.
