# 01. Specialist referral to booking

**Viewpoint:** Daughter caring for her mum (Linda, Dorothy)

**Who:** Linda, caring for her mum Dorothy. Login daughter@bench.test.

**Wants to:** Take the GP referral for a neurologist and get an appointment booked, with the referral letter and the specialist phone number to hand.

**Must see or do:** See who to call, attach the referral, book from the task, and see the phone number on the appointment.

**Plan scenario:** 1

## Steps

1. Open Tasks and find "Book a neurologist". See who to call and their number.
2. Create a task with a "who with" contact.
3. Edit the task to fix a mistake.
4. Attach the referral letter to the task or next step.
5. Record the new specialist with a phone number while booking.
6. Book the appointment straight from the task.
7. Save the booking with no date yet.
8. Set documents to bring when creating the appointment.
9. Open the appointment and see the specialist phone, email and booking link.
10. Finish with one open task, not two.

## Baseline (before any fixes)

1. FAIL. The number is only typed inside the notes text. It is not a link and the task has no contact attached.
2. FAIL. The task form has no contact box. A contact sent to the server is accepted and quietly dropped.
3. FAIL. Tasks only offer Claim and Delete. There is no edit.
4. FAIL. Neither the task form nor the next step form has a document box.
5. FAIL. The "Who to" box has no phone. Dr Anna Chen was filed with no phone at all.
6. FAIL. "Book it" only exists inside the how-did-it-go window. A task has no booking button.
7. PASS. Saves as "Not booked yet" with a Book it link on the Appointments list.
8. FAIL. No such box. Documents can only be attached after saving, from the appointment panel.
9. FAIL. The panel shows the name and practice only. No call link on the page.
10. FAIL. The GP task "Book a neurologist" and the step "See Dr Anna Chen" both stay open for the same job.
