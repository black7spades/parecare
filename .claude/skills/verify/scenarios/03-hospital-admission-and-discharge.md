# 03. Hospital admission and discharge

**Viewpoint:** Daughter caring for her mum (Linda, Dorothy)

**Who:** Linda, after Dorothy falls and is admitted for two days.

**Wants to:** Record the stay, keep the home address, change a medicine on discharge and book the follow up.

**Must see or do:** Admit and discharge dates, hospital contact, paused medicines, medicine change reason, GP follow up task, equipment return date.

**Plan scenario:** 3

## Steps

1. Record an admission with dates, hospital and ward.
2. Switch to Hospital and keep the home address.
3. Pick the hospital from the facility list.
4. Medicine alerts pause while in hospital.
5. File the discharge letter against the right place.
6. Record that Metoprolol changed and why, and from which visit.
7. A "see GP within 7 days" task appears.
8. Record loaned equipment with a return date.
